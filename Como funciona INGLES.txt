⚠️ IMPORTANT REQUIREMENT: KEEP BALANCE WITH ME

For everything to work, YOU NEED to have a balance in my bot.

Why?
When your customer buys, my bot deducts the cost from your balance
to get the product.

EXAMPLE OF WHAT DOESN'T WORK:

Customer pays: $13.00 ✅
Your bot tries to buy for $10: ❌ YOU DON'T HAVE BALANCE
Customer doesn't receive anything
YOU MUST REFUND THE CUSTOMER MANUALLY

HOW TO AVOID IT?

📊 Calculate your daily sales volume
💳 Load balance 2-3x your daily volume
🔍 Check your balance before promoting
✅ Keep a buffer always active
